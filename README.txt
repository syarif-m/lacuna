Installation
------------
Extract this ZIP into the root of your Laravel project, for example:
C:\xampp\htdocs\lacuna

The files will then be available at:
public/images/archive/<filename>

In Blade, use:
{{ asset('images/archive/01-dancing-mania.webp') }}

Notes
-----
- The Bohemian Grove owl / burning image is intentionally NOT included.
- It has been replaced by the Dancing Mania engraving supplied in the conversation.
- The other four selected images are retained: Voynich Manuscript, Antikythera Mechanism,
  Atlantis Map, and Shroud of Turin.
